# Methodology

Methodology summary for AOM Omnibus.

## Survey

- Sponsor: Verasight
- Field dates: 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-18
- Population: US adults age 18+
- Mode: online panel (Verasight Community)
- Questions: 34
- Base size: N=1000.

## Weighting

- Weight variable: weight
- CPS variables: age, race/ethnicity, sex, income, education, region, metropolitan status
- Partisanship source: Pew Research Center NPORS benchmarking surveys, three-year running average
- Vote benchmarks: Population benchmarks of 2024 vote
- Notes: June 2026 CPS benchmarks.

## Question bases and uncertainty

- Reported survey margin of error: +/-3.2%.
- Question-level sample sizes, filters and margins of error appear in `data/questions.csv`.
- Weighted toplines use the answered base for each question. Questions with filters or randomized versions can have smaller bases.
- Multi-select percentages need not sum to 100%. Missing responses are marked in `data/toplines.csv`.

Shared methodology: <https://github.com/Verasight/verasight-methodology>
